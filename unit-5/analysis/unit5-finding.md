# Unit 5 Finding — Producer Follow-up

Leadership should respond differently to producers with elevated cancellation
rates and producers with no recorded trips.

**Review elevated cancellation rates first.** Producer 11 has 5 cancellations
in 16 trips (31.25%), producer 9 has 4 in 14 (28.57%), and producer 2 has 4
in 16 (25.00%). Ask Producer Operations to review their cancellation reasons,
trip timing, and dispatch context, then speak with the producers and offer
targeted support where the review identifies a preventable issue. Track their
rates and event counts over a longer period before making consequential
decisions. These figures are signals for review, not proof that the producers
caused the cancellations.

**Resolve the no-event records separately.** Producers 14 and 15 have no trips
in the dataset, despite both being marked active. Operations should verify
their onboarding, current availability, and whether event records are
ingesting correctly. If either is not actually available, correct the roster;
if available, investigate dispatch access and offer onboarding help. The
database shows the absence of recorded trips, not the reason for it, so they
should not be treated as poor performers on this evidence alone.
